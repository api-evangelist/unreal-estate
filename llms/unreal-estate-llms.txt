# Unreal Estate

> Unreal Estate (formerly USRealty.com) is a tech-enabled flat-fee real estate marketplace and brokerage. Sellers list on their local MLS through flat-fee plans (Starter, Basic, Deluxe, and Premium, $0-$449 upfront plus 0.5% at closing) instead of paying a traditional listing commission, with service available across the US except South Dakota. The company also offers buyer, rental, and AI-powered market intelligence services, and was acquired by Abode in November 2024. Unreal Estate publishes no public developer program or API.
